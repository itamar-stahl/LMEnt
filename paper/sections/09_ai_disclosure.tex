\section*{AI Disclosure and Reflection}
We used Claude Opus and Codex Sol throughout the project. They helped rewrite
all evaluation multiple-choice questions as sentence-completion stems while
preserving their answer options, write and refactor substantial
parts of the code, provide LaTeX syntax assistance, and improve the paper's
grammar, clarity, and organization. We also used them to brainstorm and refine
evaluation and analysis ideas. The team proposed ideas independently, decided
which ideas were worth pursuing, and made all final methodological and
interpretive decisions. We reviewed AI suggestions, inspected and tested
generated code, and verified the reported results against the saved evaluation
artifacts. This assistance accelerated implementation and revision, but we
treated its outputs as drafts requiring manual verification.

While AI saved us a lot of time on basic setup, it also generated a few coding errors that slipped past us at first. 
Going back to fix those bugs after we had already moved on to the next step ended up taking way more time than expected.
