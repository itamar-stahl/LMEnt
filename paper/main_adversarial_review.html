Can Concept Erasure Reproduce Concept Exclusion? A Matched Evaluation of EMBER, RMU, and SNMF

Barak, Tabbach, Stahl, Fleisher - NLP course final project (TAU 2025/26b), mentored Project 2 - due 30 September 2026

Reviewed main.pdf (15 pages). Its source, paper/planned_evaluation_version at origin/latex_paper 1185929, compiles to identical text. It was checked against the code and result artifacts on every branch and against the NLP course project guidelines. Bibliography correctness is out of scope because it was reviewed separately.

The research is careful and its numbers reproduce, but the paper as it stands would lose substantial points: it has a visible placeholder, an unacknowledged robustness axis, a thin literature review, no code access, an undisclosed selection history and several overclaims. Almost all of this can be fixed with text and table edits before 30 September.

The study's engineering and numbers hold up well. Four matched OLMo-2 1B models were pretrained from scratch, 64 erasure candidates were built, and the final selection script is blind to the twins and test items. Nearly every figure in the paper reproduces from the committed CSV/JSON artifacts (H_test, Panels A-C, Tables 3, 4 and 12), apart from last-digit rounding. No results from different generations are mixed: every NLL/KL number comes from the accuracy-selected generation, not the superseded NLL-rule paper_tables/. The two main conclusions survive our re-checks: EMBER ends up farther from the twin than the unedited model at the question level, and high H_test does not mean twin similarity.

The paper is not ready to submit as written. Four problems will clearly cost points.
- The AI Disclosure section is still a blue 'Plan:' placeholder.
- Robustness, one of the three axes in the Project 2 brief, is neither evaluated nor acknowledged. The team's notes say the Limitations section covers it, but it does not.
- The literature review is thin: 8 references, no unlearning-evaluation work, and no citations anywhere in Sections 5-7.
- There is no access to code or models and no stated reason. Table 5 lists private cluster directory names.

Serious substantive issues:
- The selection rule was replaced after test-split twin comparisons under an earlier rule, and the paper does not say so. App. A.3 explicitly claims the opposite.
- There is no noise floor. Twins that masked an unrelated concept move the control groups as much as the matched twin, and they are as close to the Baseball and AI twins as the best erasure.
- The Conclusion calls SNMF 'consistently closest to the twin', which contradicts Sec. 5.3 and Tables 2-4.
- Several method-level claims rest on one checkpoint and a few questions. The Rome ranking depends on two questions and reverses between the selection and test splits. The AI EMBER winner is at the grid edge, 100x EMBER's own chosen delta.
- Methods are under-reported: no training recipe, erasure hyperparameters, fit data, SNMF variant or LLM judge, and no sanity checks, although most of these already exist in the repo.
- The accuracy instrument has no length-aware naive baseline. A 'pick the longest option' rule already scores 0.45-0.46 on the Baseball and AI target sets.
