\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Against matched concept-excluded models, EMBER overshoots Ancient Rome text
with a lexical damage tail, undershoots Baseball text, and approaches the AI
twin on questions only. Its Baseball parameter update is nearly orthogonal to
the exclusion displacement. Corrected RMU/SNMF probes do not reproduce the AI
question profile; their full cross-method comparison remains incomplete.
Thus erasure claims must name both the reference state and measurement level:
an efficacy score alone cannot establish that a model resembles one trained
without the concept's data.
