\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Post-training concept erasure did not consistently reproduce the behavior of
matched concept-excluded models. No method led across the three primary
criteria. RMU had the lowest absolute NLL distance for Rome; SNMF had the
lowest distance for Baseball and AI and the lowest normalized KL throughout,
but produced only modest target suppression. EMBER had the largest
target-specific NLL changes among standalone methods and the highest
efficacy--preservation score for Baseball, while RMU led for Rome. The selected
RMU+EMBER condition led for AI, but its RMU component failed the activity
check. Neither ensemble moved closer to the twin under absolute target NLL or
normalized KL distance.

A high efficacy--preservation score therefore did not imply similarity to the
concept-excluded twin. These criteria measure different properties and should
be reported separately. This conclusion is limited to three concepts, one
model family, fixed-answer evaluation, and one seed. Further work should test
additional concepts and seeds and compare erased models with their twins under
relearning.
