\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Post-training concept erasure did not consistently reproduce the behavior of
matched concept-excluded models. Under the three primary metrics, RMU had the
lowest absolute NLL distance for Rome, while SNMF had the lowest distance for
Baseball and AI and the lowest normalized KL throughout, with only modest
target suppression. EMBER produced the largest target-specific NLL changes
among standalone methods and the highest efficacy--preservation score for
Baseball; RMU led for Rome. The selected RMU+EMBER condition led for AI, but
its RMU component failed the activity check. Neither ensemble moved closer to
the twin under absolute target NLL or normalized KL distance.

A high efficacy--preservation score therefore did not imply similarity to the
concept-excluded twin. These criteria measure different properties and should
be reported separately. This conclusion is limited to three concepts, one
model family, fixed-answer evaluation, and one seed. Further work should test
additional concepts and seeds and compare erased models with their twins under
relearning.
