\section{Additional Experimental Details}
\label{app:experimental-details}

\subsection{Evidence and Reproducibility}

The training intervention is implemented in \texttt{train\_untaught.py}
within the \texttt{Untaught} framework. That directory also holds the
tracked training configurations, blacklists, and model cards. The
\texttt{ember\_eval} result notes document the Rome, Baseball, and AI
exclusion runs and the EMBER comparisons. The \texttt{mlp\_erasure} notes
document the corrected RMU and SNMF probes.
The underlying per-item outputs are stored on the cluster. The tracked result
notes report the aggregate values used in this paper.

\subsection{Scope of the Shared Comparisons}

Rome and Baseball use paired question and chunk samples for the control,
concept-excluded, and EMBER models. The AI comparison places the excluded
twin, EMBER, SNMF, and two RMU checkpoints on the same held-out question
instrument. AI's exclusion-only chunk-loss test uses a separate sample; it
cannot be interpreted as an erased-versus-excluded chunk comparison. The
Rome SNMF text probe also uses different chunks from the primary EMBER
comparison. Only Baseball has a reported whole-model directional weight
comparison in the tracked aggregate summaries.
