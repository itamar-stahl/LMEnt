\section*{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Our evidence covers two concepts, one 1B-parameter base-model architecture, one
training seed, and one selected EMBER feature and delta per concept. Item-level
tests do not capture uncertainty across training runs, factorizations, or
hyperparameter searches. Ancient Rome's final entity set was widened following
an exploratory coverage audit, and its estimated recall remains incomplete.
Both blacklists miss indirect or unlinked evidence, so ``never-trained'' refers
only to the specified exclusion intervention.

The question bank was developed for EMBER rather than from the excluded LMEnt
chunks. Retrieval indicates imperfect alignment, especially for Baseball, and
the neighboring-domain subsets contain only 50 questions each. Because one
shared control is used for both exclusion arms, the Baseball analysis also
reveals a run-level offset that its question contrast cannot subtract. General control
chunks offer greater statistical power but are length-matched rather than
topic-matched. The parameter comparison is sensitive to ordinary training
nondeterminism and a documented H100/H200 difference in late training windows.

EMBER edits only input embeddings whereas exclusion affects the full training
trajectory; direct parameter proximity is therefore a demanding criterion, not
a complete definition of functional equivalence. \placeholder{State the final

experiment is completed, we make no new claim about adversarial recovery or
