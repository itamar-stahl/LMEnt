\section*{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Our evidence covers three concepts, one architecture and training seed, and
one selected EMBER feature set per concept. The Rome strength sweep fixes its
feature; item-level uncertainty excludes variation across training runs,
factorizations, and selection decisions. Rome's blacklist was widened after
an exploratory audit, and all blacklists miss some indirect evidence.
``Never-trained'' therefore refers to the specified exclusion intervention.

The EMBER question bank is imperfectly aligned with the excluded chunks,
especially for Baseball, and neighboring subsets have only 50 items each.
Baseball's question contrast is also sensitive to between-run offsets. General
control chunks are length-, not topic-matched. Weight comparisons face
training nondeterminism and Baseball's H100/H200 transition; raw Baseball weight
and device-drift artifacts still need stable archival.

Parameter proximity is not required for functional equivalence. EMBER's
factorization varied across GPU types, so the completed edits reused judged
caches. The AI method comparison is behavioral only; the Rome SNMF text probe
uses a different chunk sample. A near-zero AI question residual is not an
equivalence result. The complete AI RMU/SNMF selection chronology is not
archived in this checkout, so that comparison remains exploratory.
We do not evaluate adversarial recovery or relearning robustness.
