\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We evaluated concept erasure against matched language models trained without
loss on entity-associated data. EMBER does not reproduce those reference states
consistently. It overshoots Ancient Rome exclusion and damages a subset of
target passages and general text; for Baseball it undershoots on held-out text
while the question instrument does not establish a concept-specific exclusion
target. Its completed weight-space update is
nearly orthogonal to the displacement produced by excluding Baseball during
training.
