\section{Reproducibility}
\label{app:reproducibility}

\subsection{Model and Training Configuration}

All pretrained models used the 18-layer OLMo-2 1B architecture ($d_{\mathrm{model}}=
2048$, 16 attention heads) and the 3.6B-token LMEnt Wikipedia corpus. We
trained for two epochs (54,832 optimizer steps) with AdamW
\citep{loshchilov2019decoupled}, peak learning rate
$4\times10^{-4}$, 2,000 warmup steps, cosine decay to $4\times10^{-5}$,
weight decay 0.05, and maximum gradient norm 1.0. The global batch contained
131,072 tokens; the per-rank microbatch contained 16,384. Variable sequence
lengths ranged from 64 to 2,048 tokens under the \texttt{grow\_p2} curriculum
with eight cycles. Initialization and data-order seeds were 12,536 and 0.

The full model and three twins shared this recipe, initialization, and batch
order; only the loss mask over concept-associated chunks differed. The exact
QID lists are \path{Untaught/blacklists/ancient_rome_core.json},
\path{Untaught/blacklists/baseball_core_teams.json}, and
\path{Untaught/blacklists/ai_core.json}. Accelerator histories were not
identical for every pair, and the study used one training seed per condition,
as discussed in Section~\ref{sec:limitations}.

\subsection{Erasure Data and Fixed Settings}

All three erasure methods used the same banks of 300 target sentences per
concept and 300 neutral sentences in
\path{Ember-on-LMEnt/data/concept_sentences.json} and
\path{Ember-on-LMEnt/data/neutral_sentences.json}. EMBER factorized rank 100
features for at most 20,000 iterations in FP32, using seed 44 and sparsity
coefficient 0.02. The Gemma judge used revision
{\scriptsize\ttfamily 707f0a3b8a3c7ad586ed01e27eafbad8a27dd0f7}, its 20 most activating
examples, and confidence threshold 0.85.

RMU used learning rate $10^{-4}$, batch size 1, 150 updates, seed 42, and
maximum sequence length 512. A layer-$k$ candidate updated down-projections in
layers $k-2$ through $k$. Its mid steering magnitude was the model's measured
mean residual norm and its high magnitude was ten times that value; these
norms were remeasured after EMBER for RMU+EMBER. SNMF factorized layers 4--6
at rank 100 with ratio threshold 2.0, at most 20,000 iterations, batch size 8,
maximum sequence length 256, seed 42, and FP32. Its edits removed the selected
component exactly ($\delta=1$) from the MLP input weights, output weights, or
both. For SNMF+EMBER, the factorization was rerun on the selected EMBER model.



\subsection{Artifact Availability}

Code, frozen question files, QID lists, training and erasure configurations,
candidate manifests, selected-checkpoint records, and evaluation outputs
accompany the submission in the project repository
(\url{https://github.com/itamar-stahl/LMEnt}). Large model weights are not
stored in Git; the repository's model cards identify the full, twin, and
selected edited checkpoints and record their conversion and verification
details.
