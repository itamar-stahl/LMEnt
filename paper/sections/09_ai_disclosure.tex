\section*{AI Disclosure and Reflection}

We used Claude Opus and Codex Sol throughout the project. They helped rewrite
the evaluation questions as sentence-completion stems while preserving the
answer options, write and refactor substantial parts of the code, provide
LaTeX assistance, and improve the paper's clarity and organization. We also
used them to brainstorm and refine evaluation and analysis ideas.
We found that they suggest too many and too similar ideas that a lot
of them didn't add "real value" to the project (in our opinion) so we 
had to carefully decide which ideas to pursue. In addition, 
while these tools accelerated implementation and revision, they also
introduced some coding errors that were discovered only after later stages had begun, requiring additional debugging and
reinforcing the need for careful manual verification.
