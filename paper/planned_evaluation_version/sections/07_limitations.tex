\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Limited computational resources restricted the study to one model
family and size, one training corpus, and one seed per condition.
We also evaluated three concepts chosen purposively, partly using
the full model's performance on selection questions. The observed
patterns may therefore vary across training runs, concepts, model
sizes, or corpora.

The configured training procedure was matched, but accelerator hardware was
not identical throughout every pair: the Rome control and twin both used an
H200, whereas the Baseball and AI twins completed part of training on other
accelerator types. Project checks found no resulting evaluation changes, but
this hardware asymmetry weakens claims that the loss mask was the only
possible source of numerical differences in those two pairs.

The concept-excluded twins are approximate references. Our selected
QIDs may miss passages about a concept that use other entities or
indirect descriptions. Conversely, a QID set may mask loss on some text
about adjacent topics. The twins therefore do not establish that the mask
eliminated every source of learning signal about the target concept.

Our evaluation measures support for one supplied correct continuation
per question. It does not measure which answer a model would generate,
and other valid wordings may receive different probabilities.
Likelihood-ranked accuracy is also sensitive to the supplied distractors
and changes in discrete steps because each split contains only 50 questions.
For Rome and Baseball, no SNMF candidate reduced target selection accuracy;
their harmonic scores were therefore zero, and the reported configuration
was fixed by the declared tie-breaking rule rather than a measured efficacy
difference.
Full-vocabulary KL captures alternative next-token probabilities
along the correct continuation, but does not follow the model after
an alternative token is chosen. These measures support conclusions
about the tested continuations rather than general claims about all
model behavior.

Finally, checkpoint selection used likelihood-ranked accuracy and a harmonic
efficacy--preservation score, so the selected checkpoints need not minimize
NLL or KL distance from the twin. SciQ provides one general-science
preservation measure, not a comprehensive capability evaluation. Each test
group contained 50 questions; the reported means and standard deviations describe
variation on those questions and do not establish that two models
behave equivalently on unseen questions.
